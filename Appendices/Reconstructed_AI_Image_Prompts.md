# Reconstructed AI Image Prompts

This appendix is a preparation file for documenting the AI-generated stimuli used in the
survey. The original image-generation prompts were not preserved. Therefore, the prompts below
are reconstructed from the final image materials and should not be treated as exact generation
records. They are intended to support transparency if a prompt appendix is added to the final
paper.

## Use Notes

- These prompts describe the apparent content, style, and intended visual function of each AI
  stimulus.
- They should be labelled as "reconstructed prompts" if included in an appendix.
- The wording may be adjusted later if the final paper needs shorter appendix entries.
- The image paths follow `Experiment/Materials_Manifest.csv`.
